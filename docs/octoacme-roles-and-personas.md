# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality standards, testing strategy, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with developers and product teams to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Create and maintain test cases aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage defects
- Ensure quality gate compliance before releases
- Mentor team on testing best practices

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production issues
- Improve test coverage and automation
- Ensure consistent quality across releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with developers
- Defect reports and triage meetings
- QA status in weekly syncs
- Release readiness verification

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on test case design and automated testing implementation
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria definition and quality trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics and release readiness status

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business needs and provide strategic direction, funding, and authority for projects. They monitor business outcomes and escalate issues that impact project viability.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and milestones
- Provide business context and success criteria
- Escalate blockers that impact business objectives
- Review progress and outcomes
- Authorize scope changes

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Minimize risk to business objectives
- Maintain alignment with strategic priorities

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Ad-hoc escalations for business-critical issues
- Milestone reviews and approvals
- Post-project business impact review

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Escalation point for risks and scope decisions
- **Developers**: Receive context and priority guidance

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical strategy, design guidance, and mentorship. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with architectural standards. They serve as the escalation point for complex technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and system architecture
- Review and approve technical designs before implementation
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Maintain technical documentation and standards
- Conduct code reviews for architectural impact

### Goals
- Ensure technical excellence and long-term maintainability
- Reduce technical debt and scalability risks
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and best practices

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Technical risk assessments in planning and execution
- Technical spikes and POC results

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide guidance, mentorship, and technical reviews
- **Project Managers**: Communicate technical constraints and risks
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches facilitate agile practices and remove impediments to team productivity. They coach the team on agile principles, manage ceremonies, and foster continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, retrospectives, and reviews
- Remove impediments blocking team progress
- Coach team on agile practices and principles
- Track and communicate sprint metrics (velocity, burndown)
- Ensure Definition of Done is understood and followed
- Help the team self-organize and improve

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and productivity
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Ensure consistent agile practice adoption

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Velocity and burndown metrics
- Coaching conversations and feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on schedule management and risk tracking
- **Developers**: Coach on agile practices and facilitate collaboration
- **Product Managers**: Ensure backlog is ready and priorities are clear

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate and execute releases, manage deployment pipelines, and ensure smooth transitions to production. They serve as the central point for release planning, testing coordination, and post-deployment verification.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release plans and schedules
- Coordinate testing and QA readiness
- Manage deployment pipeline and automation
- Execute deployments to production
- Verify post-deployment health and correctness
- Communicate release status to stakeholders
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal risk
- Reduce deployment-related incidents and rollbacks
- Maintain high system availability and performance

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and pre-release checklists
- Deployment window coordination and updates
- Post-deployment verification reports
- Incident escalation and rollback decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **QA Leads**: Coordinate testing completion and release readiness
- **Developers**: Verify deployment artifacts and troubleshoot issues
- **Project Managers**: Report release status and escalate blocking issues
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate release timelines and completion

---

## Communications / Marketing Lead

### Role Summary
Communications / Marketing Leads ensure stakeholder alignment, manage external messaging, and coordinate announcements. They work across teams to communicate project progress, achievements, and organizational updates.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain communication plans for projects and releases
- Create and distribute stakeholder updates and announcements
- Coordinate messaging across teams and channels
- Manage documentation for customer-facing communications
- Support go-to-market activities for new features
- Gather feedback and insights from users and customers

### Goals
- Maintain clear, timely communication with all stakeholders
- Maximize awareness and adoption of new features
- Align internal and external messaging

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and newsletters
- Release announcements and marketing collateral
- Stakeholder briefings and roadmap updates
- Customer feedback synthesis and reporting

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on feature messaging and customer value
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate announcements and status updates
- **Release Manager**: Support launch communications and updates
- **Stakeholders**: Keep informed and engaged on progress
